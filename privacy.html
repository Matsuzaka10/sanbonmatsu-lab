Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

"physics-shorts uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of sanbonmatsu lab to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the tool uploads videos to the owner's YouTube channel and reads basic channel and video statistics (such as view counts). It does not access data of any other users.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish videos and to measure how they perform. Access credentials are stored encrypted and are never shared, sold or transferred to anyone. No personal data of viewers is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Send a direct message to @sanbonmatsu_lab on Instagram.
